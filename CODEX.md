# FlowPilot maintainer entry point

The current engineering instructions are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and its
`engine/`, `ui/`, and `tests/` companions. The [product scope](docs/scope/index.md)
records what exists and what still needs field validation.

For a downloadable build, use the [release page](https://github.com/abishekvr0910/flowpilot/releases)
and the [artifact manifest](docs/release/RELEASE_ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.md). The
general release and pilot prerelease have different source and test evidence.
The [privacy disclosure](PRIVACY.md) states the storage and network boundaries.

Historical Codex status and benchmark notes are kept in
[docs/archive/CODEX_STATUS_2026-10-22.md](docs/archive/CODEX_STATUS_2026-10-22.md).
They do not describe the current source or release readiness.
