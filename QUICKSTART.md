# ⚡ FlowPilot 60-Second Quickstart

Welcome to FlowPilot! This guide gets you dictating in under a minute.

---

## 1. Launch FlowPilot

### If you downloaded the pre-built ZIP:
1. Extract `FlowPilot-v3.0.2-win64.zip` to any folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **`FlowPilot.exe`**.

### If you run from source:
```powershell
python desktop_app.py
```

You'll see a small frosted **microphone pill** appear near the bottom of your screen, and a FlowPilot icon in your Windows Taskbar System Tray.

---

## 2. Your First Dictation (Try It Now in Notepad!)

1. Press **`Win + R`**, type `notepad`, and hit **Enter**.
2. Press **`Ctrl + Shift + Space`** on your keyboard.
   - The microphone pill glows **Cyan** and says *"Listening..."*.
   - Any background music or video will automatically quiet down.
3. Speak a sentence naturally:
   > *"FlowPilot is working locally on my Windows machine, and it transcribes without sending my voice to the cloud."*
4. Press **`Ctrl + Shift + Space`** again (or simply stop speaking for ~1 second).
   - The pill briefly shows *"Transcribing..."* then *"Pasted!"*.
   - Your speech is instantly typed right where your cursor is!

---

## 3. Two Ways to Dictate

FlowPilot supports both popular dictation styles:

| Style | Shortcut | How it works |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Toggle Mode (Default)** | **`Ctrl + Shift + Space`** | Tap once to start speaking. Tap again when done, or simply stop talking (it auto-finalizes after an 0.85s pause). |
| **Push-to-Talk (Wispr Flow style)** | **`F9`** | Press and hold `F9`. Speak while holding the key. Release `F9` to paste instantly. |

You can switch modes anytime by right-clicking the System Tray icon and selecting **Settings**.

---

## 4. Useful Tips

- **Works Everywhere:** Dictate into VS Code, Slack, WhatsApp, Word, Gmail, browser search bars, and Discord.
- **Copy Last Dictation:** If an application ever rejects a paste, right-click the Tray icon and click **"Copy Last Dictation"** — your text is never lost!
- **Zero Privacy Worries:** 100% of the speech recognition happens on your CPU. Unplug your Ethernet / Wi-Fi and it still works flawlessly.

Enjoy fast, private dictation! 🚀
